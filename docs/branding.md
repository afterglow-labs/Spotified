# Spotified icon

The master artwork is the supplied 891 × 891 PNG with a purple musical note and the Spotified wordmark on an orange and purple background. Its original proportions and transparency are preserved in [macos/Spotified.png](../macos/Spotified.png).

| Platform | Asset | Export |
| --- | --- | --- |
| iPhone and iPad | [AppIcon.png](../ios/Resources/Assets.xcassets/AppIcon.appiconset/AppIcon.png) | Opaque 1024 × 1024 RGB PNG. The full square artwork is scaled proportionally. Colors from the artwork fill its transparent edges underneath the system mask. |
| macOS | [Spotified.icns](../macos/Spotified.icns) | The full artwork fits in an 824 × 824 area on a transparent 1024 × 1024 canvas. The ICNS contains standard and Retina representations. |
| Windows and web | [favicon.ico](../frontend/public/favicon.ico) | The full artwork is centered on a transparent square in sizes 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 128, and 256 pixels. |
