# Spotified icon

The app icon uses Afterglow's deep purple, orange, and warm ivory palette with a vinyl disc and musical note. The same artwork is used by the iOS and macOS clients.

- iOS asset: `ios/Resources/Assets.xcassets/AppIcon.appiconset/AppIcon.png`
- macOS assets: `macos/Spotified.png` and `macos/Spotified.icns`
- Generated on November 17, 2026 using the built-in image generation tool. The opaque square original was resized to 1024 × 1024 with macOS `sips`; the desktop icon sizes were packaged with `iconutil`.

## Generation prompt

> Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: production iOS app icon for Spotified by Afterglow Labs, also usable as its desktop app icon. Create one polished 1024x1024 square icon, full bleed with an opaque background and no baked-in rounded outer corners. Primary request: a distinctive minimal music emblem in Afterglow's visual style. Backdrop: extremely dark aubergine purple #120820 with a restrained soft warm afterglow behind the emblem. Subject: a bold, beautifully balanced vinyl-disc/sun emblem with a single integrated musical note formed using cream-colored negative space and a warm orange arc; clean purposeful geometry, only a few large shapes, immediately legible at small home-screen sizes. Palette: deep purple #120820, richer plum #241237, luminous orange #FF8000, warm ivory #FFF2E9. Style: sophisticated modern app identity, crisp almost-vector edges with very subtle dimensional light, premium rather than busy. Center the emblem with generous even margins, occupying about 65% of the square. No text, no letters, no app mockup, no external frame, no watermark, no blue/cyan or pink palette, no Spotify logo, no tiny equalizer bars. The image itself must be the complete square icon asset.
